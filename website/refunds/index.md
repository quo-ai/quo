# Refund policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy covers what you buy from us on the web and in the Quo desktop app: Quo Pro, Quo Sync and credit packs. These are sold through Link, Stripe’s merchant of record service, so your payment and any refund go through Link. On your bank statement the payment shows as **LINK.COM* QUO.COMPUTER**.

## 14 days to change your mind

- **Quo Pro.** Ask within 14 days of a payment (your first, or a renewal) and we refund it, less the credits you have already used from it. Each used credit counts as $0.012, the price of a credit in the 1,000-credit pack. The plan then ends.
- **Credit packs.** Ask within 14 days of buying a pack and we refund the credits you haven’t used, at the price you paid for them. If you haven’t used any, that is the full price.
- **Quo Sync on its own.** Ask within 14 days of a payment and we refund it in full. The plan then ends.

Credits are ready to use as soon as you pay, and they cost us money when you use them. That is why a refund leaves out the credits already used; you agree to this when you accept our terms at checkout. When a payment is refunded, the credits still unused from it are removed from your account.

## How to ask

- Use the receipt Link emailed you, or sign in at [link.com](https://link.com/) with the email you paid with.
- Or email [hello@quo.computer](mailto:hello@quo.computer) from the address on your Quo account, with the order number from your receipt.

Link returns the money to the card or account you paid with.

## After 14 days

You can cancel a plan at any time (**Plan & credits → Manage subscription**, or at link.com). It stops the next renewal, and the plan runs to the end of the period you paid for. We don’t refund the rest of a period after the first 14 days, and credits that have expired aren’t refunded. If something you paid for didn’t work and we couldn’t fix it, tell us and we’ll refund you.

## Premium in the phone apps

Premium bought in the Android or iPhone app is sold by Google Play or the App Store. Ask them for a refund: their own policies apply.

## Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under consumer law.
