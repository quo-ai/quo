# Brand assets

Logos, colours, screenshots and how to describe Quo.

Writing or making a video about Quo? Use anything on this page. Please don't change the logo's shape or colours, and don't use it in a way that suggests we endorse your product. Forks of the code need their own name and icon ([see TRADEMARKS.md](https://github.com/quo-ai/quo/blob/master/TRADEMARKS.md)).

## Logo

The mark is a speech bubble shaped like a Q, with an orbit around it. Use the full logo where there's room, and the mark on its own for small spaces and avatars. Leave clear space around it at least as wide as the Q's tail.

## Colour

Electric indigo on a near-white or deep ink ground is Quo. Use solid indigo for one thing per view: the main action or the mark. Ink and Ground do everything else. In the apps, Cloud marks anything that runs off the device.

** Indigo `#3B2EFF`

** Ink `#131522`

** Ground `#F9FAFD`

** Dark `#0D0F16`

** Cloud `#006D8F`

## Type

Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for everything: heavy and slightly tight for headlines, Regular for text. Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono for code and figures. Both are free (SIL Open Font License) and bundled with the apps and this site, so nothing loads from a font service.

## Name and description

- In running text, write **Quo**. In titles, store listings, handles and anywhere people search, write **Quo AI**, so Quo isn't mixed up with other products called Quo. Never “QUO”.
- **One line:** Quo is a private AI assistant that works offline.
- **Short:** Quo is a free, open-source AI assistant that runs on your own device. It works without internet, needs no account, and nothing you type leaves your device.
- **Long:** Quo runs open AI models such as Qwen, Gemma and Llama directly on your computer or phone, using llama.cpp. It's available in the browser (Chrome and Edge use your graphics card through WebGPU), as a desktop app for Mac, Windows and Linux, and on Android (iPhone coming soon), with more than 11,000 installs. Quo is open source under Apache 2.0 and made by Astra Labs Ltd in the UK.
