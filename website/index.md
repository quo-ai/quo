## Everything you need, on your device.

A complete AI assistant that runs locally, so it works anywhere and your conversations stay yours.

Answers come from the AI on your device, so Quo keeps working on planes, abroad and anywhere without signal.

## Questions.

Is Quo really free?

Yes. Everything on your device is free: every offline model, with no account and no limits. In the cloud, GPT-6 Luna is free for 20 messages a day. The most powerful models use credits, from Quo Pro ($12 a month or $99 a year, with Quo Sync) or a credit pack. On the phone, Premium is a one-time purchase. No ads. See pricing.

Do my credits work in the desktop app and Code mode?

Yes. Credits belong to your Quo account, so one balance works in Quo Web, the desktop app and Code mode. Sign in with the same email on each. The phone apps don’t use credits: their models run on the phone.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Edge on a computer: they let Quo use your graphics card, which is much faster. Firefox and Safari work but are slower. Or get the desktop app.

What do I need to run Quo?

A computer or phone from the last few years. The smallest model (about 530 MB) runs almost anywhere; bigger models want 8 GB of memory. Quo checks your device and suggests one that fits. See models.

Does anything leave my device?

Not with offline models: chats, files and voice are processed and saved on your device, and the only download is the model, once. Cloud models and your own API keys send that chat to the model’s provider, and Quo marks them clearly. With your own key it goes straight to the provider, not through Quo.

Web, desktop or phone: what’s the difference?

They’re the same private assistant. Quo Web needs nothing installed. The desktop app runs models natively on your graphics card, so it’s the fastest and fits the biggest models. Documents and voice work in all of them; photos need a model that reads images, which the desktop and phone apps have. Chats stay on each device.

My computer warns about the desktop app. Is it safe?

The app is built from the open-source code on GitHub by an automated release process. Until our signing certificates are in place, macOS may ask you to confirm it in System Settings › Privacy & Security, and Windows may show “More info › Run anyway”.

How is Quo different from ChatGPT or Claude?

Those run on company servers, so you need an account and a connection, and your chats are stored there. Quo runs on your device, so it works offline and your chats stay yours. Local models are smaller, so for the hardest tasks the big cloud models are still stronger.
