# Free on your device. Pay only for the cloud.

Everything on your device is free, with no account and no limits. The cloud is optional: one model is free every day, and credits cover the most powerful ones.

### Free

$0 On the web and desktop

- Every offline model, no limits
- Chats and memories kept on your device
- 20 free cloud messages a day with GPT-6 Luna
- Your own API keys

### Quo Pro

$12 a month, or $99 a year (save 31%)

- 1,000 credits each month
- Every cloud model, including Claude Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra
- Quo Sync included: your chats and memories on every device, end-to-end encrypted

About 150 Claude Sonnet 5.5 or 80 Claude Opus 5.5 replies a month.

### Credit packs

From $12 No plan needed

Just want Sync? $24 a year.

### Phone apps

Free Premium is a one-time $9.99

Prices may vary by country.

One credit balance for Quo Web, the desktop app and Code mode, wherever you sign in.

Buy Quo Pro, credit packs and Quo Sync in Quo Web or the desktop app, under Plan & credits. Prices include any VAT or sales tax. New accounts get 25 free credits to try, valid for 90 days. Credits cover what a cloud model costs to run, and the model menu shows the typical cost of a message. Offline models never use credits. Reply counts are estimates with thinking on, and vary with the length of a chat.
