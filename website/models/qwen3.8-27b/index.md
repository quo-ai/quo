# Run Qwen3.8 27B offline

Qwen3.8 27B is an open AI model made by Alibaba (Qwen). Quo runs it on your own computer. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

- **Good at:** The most capable model here. Reads images. For computers with 32 GB or more of memory, or a high-end graphics card.
- **Reads photos:** Yes
- **Thinks first:** Yes: it works a question through step by step, and you can read its thinking.
- **Code mode:** Good (desktop app)
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 (see [Alibaba (Qwen)’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B))

## Where it runs in Quo

| Where | Download | Memory it needs | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux) | 17.4 GB | 32 GB | Free |

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

## How to run it

### On a computer

1. Install the desktop app: [download it](https://quo.computer/#desktop), or run the one-line install shown there.
2. Open **Models & storage → Offline** and press **Download** next to Qwen3.8 27B.
3. When it’s downloaded, start a chat. It works with Wi-Fi off.

## Questions

### Does Qwen3.8 27B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, Qwen3.8 27B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

### Is it free?

Yes. In the desktop app it’s free, with no account and no limits.

### Can I use it for my own work?

Qwen3.8 27B is released under Apache 2.0. The full terms are on [Alibaba (Qwen)’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B). Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
