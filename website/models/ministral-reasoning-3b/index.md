# Run Ministral Reasoning 3B offline

Ministral Reasoning 3B is an open AI model made by Mistral AI. Quo runs it on your own Android phone. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

- **Good at:** Vision + deep reasoning and vast knowledge. Analyse images, documents, and complex problems.
- **Reads photos:** Yes
- **Thinks first:** Yes: it works a question through step by step, and you can read its thinking.
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 (see [Mistral AI’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Ministral-3-3B-Reasoning-2512))

## Where it runs in Quo

| Where | Download | Memory it needs | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Android app | 3.3 GB | Quo checks your phone | Premium (one-time) |

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

## How to run it

### On an Android phone

1. Install Quo from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quo.app).
2. Open **Settings → Models** and choose Ministral Reasoning 3B. It comes with the one-time Premium upgrade.
3. After the download, it answers with no signal.

## Questions

### Does Ministral Reasoning 3B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, Ministral Reasoning 3B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

### Is it free?

It comes with the one-time Premium upgrade in the phone app.

### Can I use it for my own work?

Ministral Reasoning 3B is released under Apache 2.0. The full terms are on [Mistral AI’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Ministral-3-3B-Reasoning-2512). Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
