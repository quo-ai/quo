# Run Ministral 3B offline

Ministral 3B is an open AI model made by Mistral AI. Quo runs it on your own browser and Android phone. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

- **Good at:** Mistral’s small model. Precise, short answers in many languages.
- **Reads photos:** Yes
- **Thinks first:** No: it answers straight away.
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 (see [Mistral AI’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Ministral-3-3B-Instruct-2512))

## Where it runs in Quo

| Where | Download | Memory it needs | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quo Web (Chrome or Edge on a computer) | 2.1 GB | 8 GB | Free |
| Android app | 3 GB | Quo checks your phone | Free |

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

## How to run it

### In a browser

[Try Ministral 3B in your browser](https://app.quo.computer/?model=ministral-3b)

1. That link opens Quo Web with Ministral 3B chosen. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
2. Press **Download Ministral 3B**. It stays in the browser for next time.
3. Chat. Nothing you type leaves the computer.

### On an Android phone

1. Install Quo from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quo.app).
2. Open **Settings → Models** and choose Ministral 3B.
3. After the download, it answers with no signal.

## Questions

### Does Ministral 3B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, Ministral 3B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

### Is it free?

Yes. In Quo Web it’s free, with no account and no limits. On the phone it’s one of the free models.

### Can I use it for my own work?

Ministral 3B is released under Apache 2.0. The full terms are on [Mistral AI’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/mistralai/Ministral-3-3B-Instruct-2512). Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
