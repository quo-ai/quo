# Run SmolLM3 3B offline

SmolLM3 3B is an open AI model made by Hugging Face. Quo runs it on your own browser and Android phone. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

- **Good at:** Hugging Face’s compact model. Writes well in six European languages, and can think first.
- **Reads photos:** No
- **Thinks first:** Yes: it works a question through step by step, and you can read its thinking.
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 (see [Hugging Face’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM3-3B))

## Where it runs in Quo

| Where | Download | Memory it needs | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quo Web (Chrome or Edge on a computer) | 1.9 GB | 8 GB | Free |
| Android app | 1.9 GB | Quo checks your phone | Premium (one-time) |

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

## How to run it

### In a browser

[Try SmolLM3 3B in your browser](https://app.quo.computer/?model=smollm3-3b)

1. That link opens Quo Web with SmolLM3 3B chosen. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
2. Press **Download SmolLM3 3B**. It stays in the browser for next time.
3. Chat. Nothing you type leaves the computer.

### On an Android phone

1. Install Quo from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quo.app).
2. Open **Settings → Models** and choose SmolLM3 3B. It comes with the one-time Premium upgrade.
3. After the download, it answers with no signal.

## Questions

### Does SmolLM3 3B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, SmolLM3 3B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

### Is it free?

Yes. In Quo Web it’s free, with no account and no limits. On the phone it comes with the one-time Premium upgrade.

### Can I use it for my own work?

SmolLM3 3B is released under Apache 2.0. The full terms are on [Hugging Face’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM3-3B). Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
