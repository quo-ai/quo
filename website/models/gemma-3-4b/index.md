# Run Gemma 3 4B offline

Gemma 3 4B is an open AI model made by Google. Quo runs it on your own Android phone. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

- **Good at:** Google multilingual all-rounder with vision. Strong quality across 140+ languages.
- **Reads photos:** Yes
- **Thinks first:** No: it answers straight away.
- **Licence:** the Gemma Terms of Use (see [Google’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3-4b-it))

## Where it runs in Quo

| Where | Download | Memory it needs | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Android app | 3.3 GB | Quo checks your phone | Premium (one-time) |

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

## How to run it

### On an Android phone

1. Install Quo from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quo.app).
2. Open **Settings → Models** and choose Gemma 3 4B. It comes with the one-time Premium upgrade.
3. After the download, it answers with no signal.

## Questions

### Does Gemma 3 4B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, Gemma 3 4B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

### Is it free?

It comes with the one-time Premium upgrade in the phone app.

### Can I use it for my own work?

Gemma 3 4B is released under the Gemma Terms of Use. The full terms are on [Google’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-3-4b-it). Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
