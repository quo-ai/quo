Run Qwen3.5 0.8B offline

Qwen3.5 0.8B is an open AI model made by Alibaba (Qwen). Quo runs it on your own browser and Android phone. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

  • Good at: Fast and light. A quick first download that runs on almost any computer.
  • Reads photos: No
  • Thinks first: Yes: it works a question through step by step, and you can read its thinking.
  • Licence: Apache 2.0 (see Alibaba (Qwen)’s page for it on Hugging Face)

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

How to run it

In a browser

  1. Open Quo Web in Chrome or Edge on a computer.
  2. Open Models & storage → Offline and download Qwen3.5 0.8B. It stays in the browser for next time.
  3. Chat. Nothing you type leaves the computer.

On an Android phone

  1. Install Quo from Google Play.
  2. Open Settings → Models and choose Qwen3.5 0.8B.
  3. After the download, it answers with no signal.

Questions

Does Qwen3.5 0.8B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, Qwen3.5 0.8B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

Is it free?

Yes. In Quo Web it’s free, with no account and no limits. On the phone it’s one of the free models.

Can I use it for my own work?

Qwen3.5 0.8B is released under Apache 2.0. The full terms are on Alibaba (Qwen)’s page for it on Hugging Face. Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
