# Run Gemma 4 E2B offline

Gemma 4 E2B is an open AI model made by Google. Quo runs it on your own computer, browser and Android phone. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

- **Good at:** Google’s small multilingual model. Reads images and speaks 140+ languages.
- **Reads photos:** Yes
- **Thinks first:** Yes: it works a question through step by step, and you can read its thinking.
- **Code mode:** Basic (desktop app)
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 (see [Google’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-E2B-it))

## Where it runs in Quo

| Where | Download | Memory it needs | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux) | 3.6 GB | 8 GB | Free |
| Quo Web (Chrome or Edge on a computer) | 2.6 GB | 8 GB | Free |
| Android app | 4.1 GB | Quo checks your phone | Premium (one-time) |

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

## How to run it

### On a computer

1. Install the desktop app: [download it](https://quo.computer/#desktop), or run the one-line install shown there.
2. Open **Models & storage → Offline** and press **Download** next to Gemma 4 E2B.
3. When it’s downloaded, start a chat. It works with Wi-Fi off.

### In a browser

[Try Gemma 4 E2B in your browser](https://app.quo.computer/?model=gemma4-e2b)

1. That link opens Quo Web with Gemma 4 E2B chosen. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.
2. Press **Download Gemma 4 E2B**. It stays in the browser for next time.
3. Chat. Nothing you type leaves the computer.

### On an Android phone

1. Install Quo from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quo.app).
2. Open **Settings → Models** and choose Gemma 4 E2B. It comes with the one-time Premium upgrade.
3. After the download, it answers with no signal.

## Questions

### Does Gemma 4 E2B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, Gemma 4 E2B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

### Is it free?

Yes. In the desktop app and Quo Web it’s free, with no account and no limits. On the phone it comes with the one-time Premium upgrade.

### Can I use it for my own work?

Gemma 4 E2B is released under Apache 2.0. The full terms are on [Google’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-E2B-it). Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
