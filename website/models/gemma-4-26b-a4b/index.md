# Run Gemma 4 26B A4B offline

Gemma 4 26B A4B is an open AI model made by Google. Quo runs it on your own computer. You download it once. After that it works with no internet, and what you type stays on your device.

- **Good at:** Google’s larger model, quick for its size: it uses only part of itself for each word. Reads images. For computers with 24 GB or more of memory.
- **Reads photos:** Yes
- **Thinks first:** Yes: it works a question through step by step, and you can read its thinking.
- **Code mode:** Basic (desktop app)
- **Licence:** Apache 2.0 (see [Google’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it))

## Where it runs in Quo

| Where | Download | Memory it needs | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Desktop app (Mac, Windows, Linux) | 15.4 GB | 24 GB | Free |

“Memory it needs” is the computer’s memory, or the graphics card’s own memory when the model runs there. A model that doesn’t fit runs slowly or not at all; Quo checks your device and suggests one that runs well.

## How to run it

### On a computer

1. Install the desktop app: [download it](https://quo.computer/#desktop), or run the one-line install shown there.
2. Open **Models & storage → Offline** and press **Download** next to Gemma 4 26B A4B.
3. When it’s downloaded, start a chat. It works with Wi-Fi off.

## Questions

### Does Gemma 4 26B A4B work without the internet?

Yes. Quo needs the internet only to download it. After that, Gemma 4 26B A4B runs on your device, and your chats, documents and photos stay there.

### Is it free?

Yes. In the desktop app it’s free, with no account and no limits.

### Can I use it for my own work?

Gemma 4 26B A4B is released under Apache 2.0. The full terms are on [Google’s page for it on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/google/gemma-4-26B-A4B-it). Quo itself is open source under Apache 2.0.
