### Quo Sync

Quo Sync keeps your chats and memories in step between your devices. It is included with Quo Pro, or can be bought on its own as a yearly plan ($24 a year).

- **Your recovery key.** Your synced chats are encrypted with a key made from a recovery key that only you hold. We can’t see it and can’t reset it. If you lose it, nobody can open the cloud copy. The chats on your devices aren’t affected, and you can delete the cloud copy and start again.
- **Limits.** One account can hold up to 250 MB of synced data and 20,000 chats. A single chat larger than about 1 MB stays on your device and isn’t synced. Photos, files and models aren’t synced.
- **It isn’t a backup.** Sync copies changes both ways, so deleting a chat on one device deletes it everywhere. Keep your own copy of anything you can’t afford to lose.
- **When a plan ends.** For 180 days you can still open Quo Sync and download your synced chats to your devices, but you can’t upload new changes. We email you once, 30 days before the cloud copy is deleted for good. Getting a plan again before then picks up where you left off.
